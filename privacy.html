Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Who uses this app

Clawd vẽ vời is used only by its owner to publish to the owner's own TikTok account (@longvachatgpt). It is not offered to the public.

Data we access

  • Basic TikTok profile info (open id, display name, avatar) to confirm the signed-in account.
  • Permission to upload and publish videos to that account (video.upload, video.publish).

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No information about other TikTok users is collected.

Deleting data

Revoke access any time in TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. Local tokens are then deleted.
